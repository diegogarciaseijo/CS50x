AI explained

Artificial intelligence is the field of computer science dedicated to creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These systems can learn from data, recognize patterns, understand language, and make decisions.

What does AI do?

Artificial intelligence allows computers to perform tasks that traditionally require human intelligence. Depending on how they are designed, AI systems can analyze information, recognize patterns, generate content, or make decisions.

Some of its capacities include:

  • Recognizing images
  • Understanding language
  • Learning from data
  • Making predictions
