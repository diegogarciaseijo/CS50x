History of Artificial Intelligence

The idea of artificial intelligence has existed for centuries, but modern AI began to develop in the twentieth century. Researchers started exploring whether computers could imitate human reasoning, eventually leading to the development of increasingly sophisticated AI systems.

The Beginning

In 1956, the Dartmouth workshop brought together researchers who helped establish artificial intelligence as a distinct field of research. The term "artificial intelligence" was used to describe the goal of creating machines capable of intelligent behavior.

Expert Systems

During the 1970s and 1980s, researchers developed expert systems that attempted to reproduce the decision-making abilities of human specialists. These systems were used in areas such as medicine and engineering.

Modern AI

In recent years, advances in computing power, large datasets, and machine learning have led to major improvements in AI. Systems can now recognize speech and images, generate text and images, and perform increasingly complex tasks.
